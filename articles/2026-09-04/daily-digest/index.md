## the day's real plot twist: everything fell over at once

this is where run-5 changed the story. on September 4, ChatGPT, Claude, and Grok all had outages the same day, with early theories pointing at shared Azure and data center problems rather than coincidence ([techweez](https://techweez.com/2026/09/04/chatgpt-claude-grok-outages/)). run-4 caught the same event but noted the root cause wasn't confirmed; treat "shared outage" as a live question, not settled fact. but three unrelated companies' flagship products going dark at once is a decent reminder that much of the AI stack sits on the same handful of cloud providers underneath the branding. if your product depends on one model vendor, build a fallback (a second provider, a cached response, a graceful degrade) the same way you'd plan for a database outage.

run-5 also picked up a quieter but consequential thread: corporate America is shifting from OpenAI and Anthropic toward open-source models, per Hacker News discussion referencing New York Times reporting, with one commenter calling models "a pure commodity" with "zero moat" ([hacker news](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49566137)). a comment thread isn't a study, but it tracks with what a lot of us feel: quality converges fast, and control over your inference stack matters more than the vendor's logo.
